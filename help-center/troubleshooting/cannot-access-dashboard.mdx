---
title: Cannot Access Dashboard
description: Resolve issues accessing your Jostter Marinho dashboard.
---

## Symptoms

- Dashboard shows blank or error
- Unable to load project overview

## Solution

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Internet Connection">
    Ensure you are connected to the internet and reload `https://josttermarinho.com/dashboard`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear Browser Cache">
    Try clearing your browser cache and cookies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try Another Browser">
    Access the dashboard using a different web browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact Support">
    If issues persist, email support@josttermarinho.com.
  </Step>
</Steps>
