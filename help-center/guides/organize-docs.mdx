## Organize Projects

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Sections">
    In your project, click "Add Section" to group related docs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Pages">
    Within a section, use "New Page" to add individual documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder Items">
    Drag and drop sections/pages to reorganize.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Changes">
    Click "Save" to apply your new structure.
  </Step>
</Steps>
