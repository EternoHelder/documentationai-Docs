## Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have:

<Callout kind="info">

- A modern web browser like Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
- An email address for account verification
- No prior setup required - everything runs in your browser

</Callout>

## Sign Up and Create Your First Space

Follow these steps to get your documentation live in minutes. Jostter Marinho handles hosting, editing, and publishing automatically.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Account" icon="user-plus">

    Visit [https://josttermarinho.com](https://josttermarinho.com) and click **Sign Up**.

    Choose your preferred method:

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Email" icon="mail">

        Enter your email and create a password. Verify your email via the link sent to your inbox.

      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Google" icon="apple">

        Click **Continue with Google** and authorize the app.

      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Documentation Space" icon="folder-plus">

    After signing in, click **New Space** on the dashboard.

    Enter a name like `My Project Docs` and select a starter template (e.g., "Blank" or "API Docs").

    Click **Create**. Your space appears in the sidebar at `https://app.josttermarinho.com/spaces/my-project-docs`.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add and Edit First Page" icon="edit-3">

    In your space, click **+ New Page** and name it `introduction.mdx`.

    Jostter Marinho uses MDX for rich docs. Paste this starter content:

````mdx
```
---
title: Introduction
description: Welcome to my docs
---

## Hello World

Your first page is live!
```
````

    Use the visual editor or switch to **Source** mode for advanced tweaks.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and Publish" icon="eye">

    Click **Preview** to see the rendered page.

    Make edits live with **Publish**. Share the URL: `https://josttermarinho.com/my-project-docs/introduction`.

    Changes auto-save and deploy instantly.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="success">

Congratulations! Your first documentation page is published. It takes under 5 minutes.

</Callout>
